Milestones

Goal

Build a no-code, modular platform where any organisation can design its workflows and get a working application, with no programming.

Context

Companies and public bodies run many internal processes by hand: requests, approvals, reimbursements, purchases. Requests arrive by e-mail or on paper, pass from person to person and are filed in shared folders.

These processes look alike everywhere: a request, a few approval steps by role, some documents and a final record.

Problem

  • No visibility. It is hard to know where a request is and who acts next.
  • No traceability. Nothing enforces the sequence or records what happened.
  • High cost. Custom software for every process is slow and expensive, and generic BPM suites need specialists.

Expected results

  • Workflow designer, execution engine and generated application
  • A first real workflow running end to end
  • A second workflow built only in the designer, proving the approach is general
  • Open-source code and documentation

Actors

  • Process owner designs the workflows
  • Requester starts a request
  • Approver validates, verifies or pays
  • Administrator deploys defiro and connects identity and other systems
